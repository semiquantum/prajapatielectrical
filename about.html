Our Story

Prajapati Electrical was founded with a clear directive: to bring high-quality, professional, and certified electrical services to the districts of Deoria and Siwan. Rooted in Rampur Bujurg, our humble beginnings as local wiring experts evolved over a decade of dedication, reliability, and precision engineering into a major B2B electrical distribution hub and contractor service.

Under the stewardship of Sohan Kumar, we expanded from small appliance repair services to large-scale residential wiring, commercial projects, industrial fittings, and solar installation programs. Today, we stand as one of the most trusted names, bridging top electrical component manufacturers with local dealers, retailers, and end-consumers.
