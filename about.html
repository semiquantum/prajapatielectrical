Our Story

Prajapati Electrical

Prajapati Electrical is a trusted and reliable electrical repair and wholesale shop located in Rampur Bujurg, Deoria, Uttar Pradesh. Founded by Sohan Kumar, we have been serving customers with dedication and quality workmanship for over a decade.

We specialize in all types of electrical repairing including AC repair, fan repair, fridge repair, house wiring, and provide wholesale electrical materials at the most affordable prices. Our skilled electricians are known for fast, reliable, and quality service.

We proudly serve customers in Rampur Bujurg, Salempur, Sohanpur, Mairwa, Siwan, Deoria and all nearby villages, delivering trusted electrical solutions right to your doorstep.
