Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: May 27, 2026

At Prajapati Electrical, we ensure quality services and products. This policy outlines our terms regarding refunds and cancellations for both services and products.

1. Service Cancellation

  • You can cancel a booked service free of charge up to 2 hours before the scheduled time.
  • If a technician has already reached your location, a minimum visit charge may apply even if you decide to cancel.

2. Service Refunds

  • If a repair fails within the promised warranty period (typically 7-30 days depending on the service) due to our workmanship, we will re-service it free of charge.
  • If the issue cannot be resolved, we may process a partial or full refund of the labor charges. Spare parts costs are generally non-refundable once installed.

3. Product Returns & Refunds

  • Unused electrical materials in original packaging can be returned within 7 days of purchase for a full refund or exchange.
  • Defective products covered under manufacturer warranty will be replaced or repaired according to the brand's policy.
  • Cut cables, used switchgears, and custom-ordered items are non-returnable.

4. Processing Refunds

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original payment method within 5-7 business days. For cash payments, refunds may be issued via bank transfer or UPI.
